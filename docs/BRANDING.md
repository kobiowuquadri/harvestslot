# HarvestSlot identity

The rounded H brings together a growing leaf and an orange connection point: harvest, coordination and a confirmed plan.

Use the shared Logo component in navigation, authentication and footers. Keep the mark and wordmark together where space permits. Use the symbol alone for app icons. Preserve proportions, leave at least one-quarter of the mark width clear around it, and use a white backing on dark surfaces.

Palette: forest green #137A3B, harvest orange #F28A48, ink #101828, white #FFFFFF. Orange is a decorative accent; green remains the primary action color. Typography: Poppins semibold for the wordmark and headings, Inter for interface text.
