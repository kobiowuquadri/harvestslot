| 33 | 2026-11-18 | **Simplified sale journey.** Public registration offers two ways to start (*Sell cassava* / *Buy cassava*); transporter, driver and cooperative sign-up and personas leave the public flow and the demo, while existing accounts, permissions and records keep working. Each offer answers “How will the cassava get there?” (`COLLECT_FROM_FARM` or `FARMER_DELIVERS`, with any delivery cost the farmer pays shown before acceptance); an offer without that answer can only be a private **draft**, which reserves nothing. A booking is *Ready to harvest* when both sides agreed the current terms, capacity is reserved, the handover is complete and the booking is valid — the new `HANDOVER_AGREED` check replaces the transport and driver checks, which remain only for older bookings with transport terms. Accepting an offer with a handover confirms the booking at once. Transport quotes are refused for handover bookings. Receiving for these bookings is one step (weight received and accepted), issuing the produce invoice immediately. Every harvest and booking carries a plan sentence and a `nextStep`. | The farmer's question is “Who will buy my cassava, how much will they take, and when and where should it be ready?”. Freight quotes, vehicle holds and driver confirmation added steps and failure points that a buyer collecting from the farm, or a farmer delivering, does not need. Keeping the transport code for historical bookings avoids rewriting records. Material changes still need re-agreement, and double-booking prevention (slot row locks) is unchanged. |
